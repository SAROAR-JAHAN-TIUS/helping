3. What is currently broken

These are the things we should treat as bugs, not new features.

A. Multi-participant WebRTC

Current target:

Participant A
      ↕
Participant B
      ↕
Participant C
      ↕
Participant D
...
      ↕
Participant J

Currently:

2 participants can work
3+ participants have connection problems
some participants show Connecting...
sometimes Disconnected
participant tiles don't reliably establish media

This is the biggest frontend logic problem.

B. Leave button

The Leave button must do:

Click Leave
     ↓
Stop local media
     ↓
Close peer connections
     ↓
Close WebSocket
     ↓
POST /meetings/{id}/leave
     ↓
Navigate to Dashboard

It must not trigger meeting processing.

C. End Meeting

This is where your previous logic got confused.

Ending a meeting should be:

Host clicks End Meeting
          ↓
Tell backend meeting is ended
          ↓
Backend stops/finalizes meeting
          ↓
All participants receive meeting-ended signal
          ↓
Everyone leaves WebRTC
          ↓
Everyone leaves WebSocket
          ↓
Frontend goes to FINAL/PROCESSING page
          ↓
Backend processing finishes
          ↓
Results available
          ↓
AskAI / PDF / Meeting Summary

The critical point:

End Meeting ≠ Leave Meeting ≠ Process Meeting ≠ Dashboard.

They are separate states.

4. Your meeting state should become very clear

Use something like:

WAITING
   ↓
ACTIVE
   ↓
ENDING
   ↓
PROCESSING
   ↓
COMPLETED

And for a participant:

JOINED
   ↓
IN_MEETING
   ↓
LEFT

This prevents the current mess where clicking End can accidentally trigger processing while the frontend is still sitting inside the meeting room.
